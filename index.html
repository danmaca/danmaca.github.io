Filament Stock

A personal inventory of 3D-printing filaments.

Filament Stock keeps track of the filaments of its owner: brands, materials, finishes and colors, the physical spools (status, remaining weight, storage location, price), the orders they were bought by and photos of the filaments and of the prints. It can also import the spools from an order in an online shop.

The application runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up and there are no other users.

Why Filament Stock asks for access to Google Drive

The application can upload the backups of its database to a folder in the owner's Google Drive, so that a copy of the data is kept outside the computer. When Google Drive is connected, the application asks only for the permission drive.file – access only to the files used with this application:

  • it sees only the folder chosen in the Google Drive folder picker and the backup files it uploaded itself; it cannot see or read any other files in the Drive,
  • it uploads new backup files to the folder and lists the folder to find out which backups are uploaded already,
  • it moves its own older automatic backups to the Google Drive trash, so that only the configured number of them is kept,
  • it reads the e-mail address of the Google account to show which account is connected.

The access can be revoked at any time by the Disconnect button of the application or in the Google Account settings. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
